# U.S. Superstore Sales Analysis

A class project reviewing U.S. Superstore sales from 2014 through 2017. The project workbook retains the supplied practice sheets and adds a finished report dashboard, formula-linked summaries, and geographic comparisons.

## Project files

- `Final_Report.pdf` - four-page, presentation-ready report with charts and written findings.
- `Final_Report.docx` - editable Word version of the report.
- `Sales_Analysis_Project.xlsx` - completed Excel project workbook.
- `smpl_original.xlsx` - unchanged copy of the supplied workbook.
- `images/` - chart images used in the report.

## Workbook guide

- `Sheet5` - final report dashboard with eight charts: all six requested analyses, plus top states and top city-state markets.
- `Analysis` - formula-linked regional, segment, product, customer, state, city-state, yearly, monthly, and quarterly summaries.
- `Data` - source sales rows.
- The remaining original practice sheets are kept in the workbook.

## What the data shows

- Total sales: **$2,297,201**; total profit: **$286,397**; units sold: **37,873**.
- The West leads regions with **$725,458** in sales.
- Consumer customers contribute **$134,119**, or **46.8%**, of profit.
- The top product by sales is the Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier (**$61,600**).
- Sean Miller is the top customer by sales (**$25,043**).
- Annual sales rise from **$484,247** in 2014 to **$733,215** in 2017.
- November has the most sales when each calendar month is combined across the four years (**$352,461**).
- California leads state sales (**$457,688**); New York City, New York leads city-state sales (**$256,368**).

## Method

The `Data` sheet has 9,994 transaction lines. Sales and profit are summed at the line level. Product and customer rankings use total sales. Customer segment contribution uses total profit. City totals are grouped by both city and state to keep identically named places separate. Monthly totals combine the same calendar month across 2014-2017; the workbook also retains month-by-year and quarterly summaries.

Amounts are U.S. dollars. The top-ten lists reflect the supplied dataset snapshot.
